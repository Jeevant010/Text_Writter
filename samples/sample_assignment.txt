# Problem: 1     State (i) tensor order, (ii) its shape and (iii) the total number of entries

a) \to A Scalar a \in R
   i) \to tensor order is 0
   ii) \to Shape: (), an empty tuple
   iii) \to Total number of entries: 1

b) \to A vector x \in R^{7}
   i) \to tensor order is 1
   ii) \to Shape: 7 or (7,1) tuple as represented as Matrix.
   iii) Total number of entries: 7

c) \to A matrix A \in R^{4\times6}
   i) \to tensor order is 2
   ii) \to shape: (4,6)
   iii) Total number of elements: 4 \times 6 = 24

d) \to A tensor of x \in R^{3\times5\times2}
   i) \to Tensor order is 3
   ii) \to Shape: (3, 5, 2)
   iii) \to Total number of entries:-
            3 \times 5 \times 2 = 30

e) \to A tensor Y \in R^{8\times3\times32\times32}
   i) Tensor order is 4
   ii) Shape: (8, 3, 32, 32)
   iii) Total number of entries:-
            8 \times 3 \times 32 \times 32 = 24576

# Problem: 2     Tensor order & Number of entries in a tensor

\to Tensor order is the number of independent axis or indices required to identify a single element in the tensor
         T \in R^{d1 \times d2 ... dN}, order is N

\to Number of entries is the number of scalar elements contained in the tensor, defined as:
         T \in R^{d1 \times d2 ... dN}

   Total entries:- d_1 \times d_2 ... d_N = \prod d_k (k=1 to N)
